Common questions

How does earning time work?

Start a timer for something useful, like reading or studying. When the session ends, that time goes into your bank. Starting a fun activity, like social media or games, spends it. You can give an activity its own rate, so a workout can earn 1.5× the time.

My steps aren't adding time.

Open the Health app, go to Sharing › Apps › Joyhour and turn on Steps. Then make sure the step goal is turned on in Joyhour's settings.

How do I lock apps until I start a fun session?

Edit a fun activity and choose the apps to lock. Joyhour asks for Screen Time access the first time. The apps stay locked and open only while a session for that activity is running.

The Apple Watch app is empty.

Open Joyhour on your iPhone once. The watch gets its data from your phone.

How do I delete my data?

Everything Joyhour stores lives on your devices. Swipe to delete single entries in History, use Reset everything in Settings to start over, or delete the app to remove all of its data.
